## 1. Brand Voice & Editorial Philosophy

At **Hotel Waidhan Singrauli BNB**, our words must reflect our service: **honest, dependable, and genuinely helpful**.

### Rules of Engagement:
1. **Speak Directly & Clearly:** Use active voice. Avoid flowery Victorian phrasing (*"a tradition of gracious living"* — which competitors copied from 1990s brochures). Speak directly to the engineer, the solo bus passenger, or the corporate visitor.
2. **Zero Faux-Superlatives:** Never claim to be the *"#1 luxury resort in India"* when the property is an accessible, high-comfort town-center hotel. Let our 4.8★ reviews, 24/7 power backup, and transparent ₹349 pricing speak for themselves.
3. **Natural Keyword Integration:** Keywords like *"Hotel in Waidhan"* or *"Deluxe AC room near Waidhan Bus Stand"* must flow naturally within sentences, answering real traveller questions rather than stuffed into meaningless tag clouds.
4. **Transparent Facts Over Guesswork:** If a specific room dimension or policy is pending owner confirmation, use explicit brackets like `[CONFIRM ROOM DIMENSION]` rather than inventing numbers.
