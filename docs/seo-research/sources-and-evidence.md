## 2. Research Limitations & Unverified Information

To maintain the highest professional standard of technical accuracy, the following boundaries and limitations are explicitly declared:

1. **Private Competitor Analytics & Revenue Data:**
   * Competitor organic click counts, bounce rates, daily unique visitors, conversion rates, and revenue figures are private data housed inside Google Analytics and Google Search Console. Any third-party tool claiming exact traffic figures for small regional properties in Waidhan provides statistical extrapolations, not verified facts.
2. **Search Volume Verification:**
   * Search volumes for hyper-local regional queries (e.g., *"Dormitory in Waidhan"*, *"Hotel near Waidhan Bus Stand"*) are low-volume long-tail terms. Mainstream keyword tools (Semrush, Ahrefs, Google Keyword Planner) frequently report these as `0 - 10` or `Low Volume` due to sample-size thresholds in Tier-3 Indian cities. However, Google Autocomplete, Related Searches, and live directory rankings prove that real searchers actively execute these queries. Volumes in this report are marked as conservative ranges (`Est`) based on regional industrial demographics and transit volumes.
3. **Keyword Difficulty Scores:**
   * Global SEO software calculates Keyword Difficulty based on top-ranking backlink profiles (which are often OTAs like MakeMyTrip or Booking.com). This artificially inflates global KD scores. In local SEO, localized relevance, NAP consistency, and proximity routinely outrank national OTA pages for high-intent searchers.
4. **Third-Party Backlink Metrics:**
   * Paid backlink suites (Ahrefs/Majestic) have limited crawler frequency in regional Indian web ecosystems. Competitor backlink profiles documented in this research reflect publicly discoverable citations and directory references rather than proprietary database dumps.
5. **Dynamic Search Personalization:**
   * Google search results vary depending on whether the searcher is physically in Waidhan, Singrauli district, elsewhere in Madhya Pradesh, or outstation (e.g., searching from Delhi, Varanasi, or Mumbai). Local Pack results prioritize distance from the searcher's physical GPS coordinates.
