## 1. Executive Research Summary

An exhaustive audit of Google Search, Google Maps Local Pack, Government Directories (`singrauli.nic.in`), Online Travel Agencies (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda, Booking.com), and business directories (Justdial, Magicbricks) was conducted across 15 search queries.

### Key Market Findings:
1. **Severe Website Deficit Among Direct Competitors:** Out of 15+ identified accommodation businesses in Waidhan, only **3 properties** maintain official standalone domains (`hotelsatyainternational.com`, `hotelthegrandagastya.com`, and `singraulipalace.com`).
2. **Critical Competitor Vulnerabilities:**
   - **Singrauli Palace Heritage Hotel** (`singraulipalace.com`), widely cited as the top property in the area, currently returns a fatal `Error establishing a database connection` (verified Oct 22, 2026).
   - **Hotel Satya International** (`hotelsatyainternational.com`) suffers from severe on-page SEO flaws: 14+ conflicting `<h1>` tags on a single page, complete absence of a meta description, spelling errors in headings (*"Presidencial"*, *"Bar & Restaurent"*), and zero location modifiers in its title tag.
   - **Hotel The Grand Agastya** (`hotelthegrandagastya.com`) contains a duplicate `<title>` tag repeated 3 times in raw HTML, zero meta description, and 5 separate `<h1>` tags.
   - **Hotel Raj Kamal** has an expired/unresolved DNS domain (`hotelrajkamalsingrauli.com`).
3. **Heavy OTA Dependency & Commission Leakage:** Leading competitors like **Hotel Udika Palace**, **Hotel Pratap Palace**, and **Hotel The Lord** have **no standalone websites whatsoever**. They rely 100% on MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda, and Justdial, sacrificing 18% to 25% in commissions.
4. **Completely Uncontested Niches:**
   - **Dormitory Accommodation (₹349–₹499/day):** No competitor has an indexed, SEO-optimized page for single dormitory beds or transit bunk accommodation.
   - **Monthly PG Accommodation (₹4,500–₹7,000/mo):** Captured solely by unstructured Justdial/Magicbricks listings without branded websites.
   - **Corporate Stays Near NTPC / NCL with Instant GST Billing:** Under-optimized across all local competitors.
